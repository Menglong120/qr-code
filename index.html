Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about customizing, generating, and printing QR codes.

Do these QR codes ever expire or stop working? ▼

No! All QR codes generated here are static QR codes. The destination URL or data is directly encoded within the QR matrix pattern itself. Since there are no intermediate redirect servers or expiring tracking databases, your QR codes will remain fully functional forever.

How do I create a Wi-Fi QR code so guests connect automatically? ▼

Click on the "Wi-Fi Network" tab above, enter your Wi-Fi SSID (network name) and password, and choose WPA/WPA2/WPA3. When guests scan the code using the native camera app on iOS or Android, a popup prompt will appear allowing them to connect with a single tap without manually typing passwords.

Can I add my business logo without affecting camera scannability? ▼

Yes! QRCraft Studio is configured with Level H (High) Reed-Solomon Error Correction by default. This reserves up to 30% redundant code capacity, allowing you to position your company logo in the center while maintaining 100% instant readability across all smartphone cameras.

Which format should I use for printing on merchandise and posters? ▼

We recommend downloading the SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) format for professional graphic design and print production. SVG files use mathematical vectors rather than pixels, ensuring clean, sharp edges regardless of magnification. Alternatively, you can download a massive 4096px Ultra-HD PNG.

Is QRCraft Studio truly free to use for commercial projects? ▼

Yes, 100% free! You can generate, customize, and download unlimited QR codes for personal use, retail packaging, client projects, digital marketing campaigns, and print media with zero royalties or hidden subscription fees.
